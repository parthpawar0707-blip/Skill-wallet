## Submission Tips for Highest Evaluation Marks
1. **Resolution:** Capture at full screen (1920×1080) so labels and metrics are sharp and legible.
2. **Naming Convention:** Save files with descriptive names:
   * `01_Dataset_Raw.png`
   * `02_Tableau_DataSource.png`
   * `03_Calculated_Fields.png`
   * `04_Worksheet_Scatter_Plot.png`
   * `05_Master_Dashboard.png`
   * `06_Dashboard_Interactive_Filter.png`
   * `07_Tableau_Story_Scene.png`
   * `08_Tableau_Public_Live.png`
   * `09_Flask_Web_Integration.png`
   * `10_GitHub_Repo_Evidence.png`
3. **No Watermarks / Cropping:** Ensure window borders or browser URL bars are included to prove genuine live execution.
