The 8 Required Tableau Visualizations

  • Viz 1: Impact of Study Hours on Academic Performance (Bar chart grouped by study intensity colored by average stress)
  • Viz 2: Gender and Their Depression Level (Horizontal comparative bar chart evaluating depression severity)
  • Viz 3: Study Hours vs Academic Performance (Disaggregated scatter plot with linear trendline and risk classification)
  • Viz 4: Average Stress Level by Gender (Vertical bar chart monitoring subjective stress ratings)
  • Viz 5: Analyzing Heart Rate Variability (Biometric HRV analysis across mental health risk tiers)
  • Viz 6: KPI Overview Cards (High-level institutional scoreboard: Headcount, Stress, Sleep, Performance)
  • Viz 7: Mental Risk Level Distribution (Donut / Pie chart with percentage breakdown)
  • Viz 8: Academic Performance Breakdown (Student distribution across academic performance tiers)
